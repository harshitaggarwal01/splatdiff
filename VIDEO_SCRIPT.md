# Second Look — 3-minute demo script (v3)

Total running time: 3:00 maximum. Narrate plainly; let the screen carry the detail. Max 2 jargon terms on camera ("grounded or dropped" is the one to keep). Buttons on screen: *Use the sample room*, *Check the room*, *Past checks*, *Speak your care notes*.

**Tip before recording:** replay the armchair run from *Past checks* so the ink-strike always appears on cue, even if a fresh live run behaves differently on the day.

**0:00–0:15 — Hook, over the struck armchair.** Open on the report with the armchair claim already struck through, before/after frames behind it. "Last month Dad's room was set up safe. This month the walker is out of reach — and nobody noticed. Dad is 82, he lives alone, and about 1 in 4 adults over 65 falls each year. Drift can create hazards. This is Second Look: film the room when you visit, and we show you what drifted."

**0:15–0:45 — The ink-strike hero.** Stay on the struck claim; let the ink-strike animation replay. "The AI said an armchair appeared. It didn't — SAM's re-check found it in the first video, at confidence 0.75. Video tracking had missed it there; the image re-check overturned the claim before it reached the family." Point at the reason line under the strike. "Struck, reason shown. That is the whole product in one moment: every finding shows its evidence, or it is struck from the report in front of you." One line on the test room, on screen as a label: "Synthetic test room (Muse Image) — real footage works the same way." The sample pair (`data/sample_before.mp4`, `data/sample_after.mp4`) changed exactly three scoreable things: the blue backpack was removed, the floor lamp moved next to the armchair, and a red suitcase appeared.

**0:45–1:00 — Real phone footage beat.** [PLACEHOLDER: user's real clips] Show 10–15 seconds of a real handheld phone walkthrough pair — a real room, a lamp and a bag moved between clips. On-screen label throughout: "Synthetic test room (Muse Image) — real footage works the same way" applies to the sample segments; label this beat "Real phone footage". Voiceover, one breath: "No camera watching him sleep, no hardware to install. You film for 20 seconds when you visit, with Dad; nothing is recorded between visits."

**1:00–1:20 — Voice note to watchlist.** Open the Care dossier UI at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`. Click *Use the sample room*, then *Speak your care notes* and play the spoken care note: "Please keep an eye on the walker — it must stay next to the bed." Muse Voice Transcribe (`muse-voice-transcribe-1.0`) transcribes it (a 10-second note takes about 2.3 s), and Muse Spark turns it into a watchlist item: *walker — must stay put*. "Her hands were holding the phone, filming. The watchlist is spoken, not typed. Watchlist items are always tracked and always reported first."

**1:20–1:45 — Live check, compressed, plus vocabulary repair.** Click *Check the room*. Do not narrate the plumbing — let the chips fill in, accelerating: normalising, keyframes, inventory, tracking, re-checks, judging, reporting. One line only: "Spark inventories the room; SAM tracks every object in both videos; anything that seems to appear or vanish gets a second look." Then slow down for the walker chip: "Here SAM didn't know the word *walker* — it returned nothing. Spark taught it a new phrase, 'metal walking frame', SAM re-ran, and found it at confidence 0.875. The models need each other: one reasons, one proves."

**1:45–2:25 — Report: HIGH finding, evidence, next step.** Open the finished report. Watchlist first: walker — *in place*, with its status in plain language (*"Seen where it should be. We can't check it's reachable or working."*). Then the HIGH finding (floor lamp moved / walker-class finding as the run presents it), high-risk first. Open the evidence board and show one finding up close: before and after frames with the SAM box drawn, frames seen, confidences. Then land on the block under the HIGH finding: **Do this next** — "Move the walker back within arm's reach of the bed. Ask Dad to show you he can reach it. Then re-film this corner — we'll confirm it's back." Tap **Mark as sorted**. "A report that ends at detection is a demo. This one ends at: here is what to do, tell us when it's done, and we re-check it next visit." If an item shows `not_seen`, say the line printed under it: "Not seen in this video — not the same as missing. Check before you worry."

**2:25–2:45 — Honest numbers.** Show the eval summary once, no ticker. Say it exactly like this: "On our one scoreable test room (3 runs), the raw model invented claims 25–40% of the time; grounding struck every one — a smoke test, not a benchmark. Recall on a real, cluttered room is untested, and a missed walker matters more than a false alarm. A fresh check takes about a minute and about $0.33; replaying a past check is free."

**2:45–3:00 — Close: 3 run it, 2 built it.** "Three Meta models run every check: Muse Spark reasons, SAM 3.1 proves, Muse Voice Transcribe listens. Two more built it: Muse Image made our scoreable synthetic test room, and Muse Wonder wrote the codebase — about 13 reviewed generation rounds, parallel workers, transcripts and even the red-team critique in `build_log/`. Second Look is not a medical device — it reports what drifted, with receipts, or it strikes the claim. Second Look: what changed in Dad's room?"
